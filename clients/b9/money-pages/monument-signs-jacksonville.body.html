What is a monument sign?

A monument sign is a low-profile, ground-level sign — typically masonry, stone, or an aluminum-and-composite structure — that sits at the entrance to a business, office park, community, or campus. Unlike a tall pole sign, a monument sits close to the ground, reads as permanent and premium, and reinforces your brand the moment someone arrives. It's the highest-impact piece of signage most businesses and communities ever buy.

Types of monument signs we build

  • Commercial & business entrance monuments — a branded first impression at your storefront, office, or building entrance.
  • Community & HOA entrance monuments — the entrance statement for new residential communities and neighborhoods, coordinated with builder and developer timelines. See our homebuilder & community signage →
  • Corporate campus & multi-tenant monuments — directory-style monuments that list multiple businesses at one address.
  • Illuminated monuments — ground-lit, halo-lit, or internally illuminated push-through letters so your entrance works after dark.

What drives the cost of a monument sign in Jacksonville?

Most monument signs in the Jacksonville area run $8,000–$35,000+ installed. A single-face masonry monument with dimensional lettering typically lands in the $12,000–$20,000 range; larger dual-entry, illuminated, or landscaped monuments go higher. Price is driven by:

  • Size & structure — brick/stone or precast vs. an aluminum-and-composite build.
  • Lettering — flat-cut acrylic vs. dimensional metal vs. push-through illuminated. Explore dimensional & channel letters →
  • Lighting — non-illuminated, ground-lit, or internally illuminated (adds electrical and transformers).
  • Permitting & engineering — Duval, Clay, and St. Johns each have their own sign codes, and Florida requires wind-load engineering.
  • Site work — footings, grading, and landscape/irrigation coordination at the entrance.
