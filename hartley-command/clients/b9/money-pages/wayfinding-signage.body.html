What a wayfinding system includes

  • Vehicular directionals — entrance, exit, and route signs sized for traffic speed and distance.
  • Pedestrian & site directories — maps and multi-destination signs that orient visitors on arrival.
  • Building ID & interior wayfinding — door, suite, floor, and directory signage. See commercial signage →
  • ADA-compliant signage — code-correct tactile and Braille signage where required.

Wayfinding is a system, not a sign

The value of wayfinding is consistency: a single visual language — materials, color, typography, and iconography — repeated at every decision point so visitors move without thinking. We plan the whole path first, then design signage that shares one identity with your monument and building signs, so the entire property reads as one intentional brand. What wayfinding costs

Because wayfinding is a program, pricing is scoped to the property — an individual directional may be a few hundred dollars, while a full campus or community system is a coordinated package. We start with a sign plan and a phased budget so you can build it in stages if needed.
