# READ: Rembrand Koning on AI Performance Boosts

**Type:** read  
**Author/Speaker:** Rembrand Koning  
**Date:** 2026  
**Source:** https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/how-ai-helps-the-best-and-hurts-the-rest/  

## Summary

A field experiment with Kenyan entrepreneurs found that generative advice raised revenue and profit for already-strong owners by roughly 15 percent, while weaker performers saw a decline of about 10 percent. The difference came down to judgment in filtering generic or incorrect guidance.
