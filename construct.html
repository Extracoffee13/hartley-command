operating principle

Operator cap at seven. Adding an eighth requires retiring one. This forces discipline and prevents the Cyndra trap of hiring agents instead of compounding the ones you have. Custom Operators (DeckForge, B9 Drones) live outside the cap because they're client-specific, not standard kit.

Operating discipline — quality gates

Every Operator output passes a discipline gate before it ships. The gates are productized — they show up in PRAGMA's client deliverables as "this passed the Quality Triangle" / "this passed the Creative Board." Competitors don't gate. PRAGMA does. That's the credibility moat.

▦ Quality Triangle
Skeptic · Architect · Security
Already live inside the Construct (originated in construct-forge, now in praxis/quality_triangle.py). Applies to every strategy, ops, finance, research, or analysis deliverable. Skeptic pressure-tests the claims. Architect checks it fits the client's actual operating model. Security screens for legal / financial / brand / operational risk. Fails → log, don't ship.
▦ Creative Board
Vision · Craft · Brand-fit
Vector's equivalent of Quality Triangle for visual / creative deliverables. Vision — does it carry the core idea? Craft — typography, color, motion, layout, execution quality. Brand-fit — does it match the institutional voice + locked design system? Applies to logos, decks, motion graphics, website builds, social graphics, sign proofs. Fails any pillar → log, don't ship.

The Self-Improving Loop

Every Operator routes its corrections, mistakes, and lessons through the Self-Improving Loop. Same loop, every Operator. Corrections become updates; updates compound.

When Bobby corrects an Operator's output, the correction routes through the Loop and updates the relevant skill or memory file. Operators that get corrected don't repeat mistakes; Operators that don't get corrected drift. The Loop is non-negotiable in every PRAGMA engagement — without it, the system degrades.
