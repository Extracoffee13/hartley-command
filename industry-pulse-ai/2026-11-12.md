Brand 9 Signs: indirect.

**PRAXIS lesson:** Adding agents or voters protects against the worst agent but doesn't make the system better than the best one, so measure each agent on the real task mix before you build a swarm. Spend the effort on giving the best agent better tools, such as a licensed voice MCP, rather than on more agents.
