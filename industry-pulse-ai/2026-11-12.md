4. **arXiv 2611.12953: agent architecture cost-success trade-offs across 13 enterprise SOPs** ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12953)) — A controlled comparison of single-agent, skill-augmented, multi-agent orchestration and swarm designs on 13 enterprise-style SOPs finds no universal best architecture; multi-agent and skill-augmented designs used 1.4 to 4.7 times the tokens of a single agent, and a redesigned multi-agent arm reached 75.6% success versus 67.4% for the baseline. **Applicable: YES** — The Construct: pick the architecture per workflow and log tokens per successful task, not just success rate; extra agents have to earn their 1.4-4.7x cost. PRAGMA: a cost-per-success benchmark to bring to client architecture reviews. Brand 9 Signs: indirect.

**PRAXIS lesson:** Multi-agent and skill-heavy designs cost 1.4 to 4.7 times more tokens and only sometimes win, so choose the architecture per workflow by cost per successful task. Wherever an unattended loop touches someone's money or livelihood, keep a human sign-off and disclose the AI, which Anthropic's policy now requires from today.
