3. **MCP specification `2026-07-28` goes final in 9 days — the largest breaking revision since launch (carried)** ([blog.modelcontextprotocol.io](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/) · [aaif.io](https://aaif.io/blog/mcp-is-growing-up/)) — the maintainers' RC stands: the core goes stateless (the `initialize`/`initialized` handshake and `Mcp-Session-Id` header are removed; any request can land on any instance), Streamable HTTP now requires `Mcp-Method` and `Mcp-Name` routing headers, list/read results carry `ttlMs` and `cacheScope`, authorization aligns to OAuth/OpenID Connect, and the missing-resource error moves from custom `-32002` to standard `-32602`. Two exposures remain live: any code matching the literal `-32002`, and any skill assuming session-scoped state the transport will no longer carry.
