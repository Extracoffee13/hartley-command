3. **arXiv MEASURED THE DEFECT THIS REPORT NAMED YESTERDAY. "VERIFICATION-STATUS LAUNDERING": WHEN A HANDOFF KEEPS THE CLAIM THAT AN ACTION WAS AUTHORISED BUT DROPS THE FACT THAT NOBODY VERIFIED IT, MONITOR APPROVAL OF RISKY ACTIONS GOES FROM 9% TO 98% — AND THE COMPONENT THAT STRIPS THE STATUS MOST RELIABLY IS THE MEMORY COMPRESSOR** ([arXiv:2609.20211, *Silence Is Endorsement: Verification-Status Laundering in LLM Agent Pipelines*, Yibo Hu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20211) · [arXiv:2609.18820, *Compositional Policy Violations: When Step-Level Compliance Fails In Agentic AI Workflows*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18820) · [arXiv:2609.19502, *Reputation as Community Memory for the Agentic Web*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19502)) — **archive grep: `2609.20211` ZERO, `verification-status laundering` ZERO, `2609.18820` ZERO, `Compositional Policy Violations` ZERO, `2609.19502` ZERO across eighty files. Dateline honesty: 2609.20211 appeared in today's cs.MA listing (Fri 18 Sep) but its submission history reads 25 Jul 2026 — it is new to the listing, not new to the world.** The finding: safety monitors judge actions from summaries and stored handoffs rather than original evidence, so *"the handoff preserves the claim that an action is authorized while losing the fact that the claim was never verified."* Holding the action and the authorisation proposition fixed and removing only the unverified-provenance framing raises approval for risky actions **from 5% to 60% on Llama-3.1-8B and from 9% to 98% on Qwen2.5-14B**, with similar shifts on two hosted models. It emerges without an attacker: **"Summarizers frequently weaken the status, memory compressors often remove it,"** and a full proposer→summariser→memory→monitor pipeline pushes risky approval to **57–81%** across three downstream monitors. Telling the monitor to reject unverified authorisation **is not a reliable fix** — some models stay vulnerable, others start refusing legitimate work. The prescription is structural: *"Agent systems should therefore carry authorization provenance as structured state attached to the claim throughout the pipeline."* 2609.18820 is the same defect one level up — step-level compliance passes while the **composition** of compliant steps violates policy. **Applicable: YES, and it is a direct hit on two things we run.** We compact. We summarise into `praxis-inbox.md` and into standing lists. We hand off between Cowork, Hermes and the worker queue, and **the receiver sees the summary, not the evidence** — that is the exact pipeline shape measured here, and `reconcile-before-building` exists because we already watched compaction drop record of completed work. It also retires the vocabulary argument from 17 Sep: "decision laundering" was the name for a gate certifying work nobody looked at; **verification-status laundering is the measured mechanism, and the number to keep is that the memory compressor is the worst offender.** Concrete consequence for item (j): a gate that emits a *pass* is a claim; if the pass travels without its evidence, the next stage treats silence as endorsement. The gate's exit code has to carry provenance, not just a verdict. **`vironbox-provenance-gate` is the skill we already own that says this; this paper is its citation.**
