# Industry Pulse — AI / Agent Ecosystem · 2026-09-23

Daily scan for The Construct. Source URL · one-line summary · applicable to The Construct.

1. **OpenAI ships GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna at roughly half the price of GPT-5.6** ([source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/)) — Released 22 Sep: Sol at $2/$10 and Luna at $0.10/$0.50 per Mtok as permanent pricing, with OpenAI claiming Sol makes about half as many factual mistakes as its predecessor; live in API, Codex and ChatGPT. **Applicable: MAYBE** — Luna is a cheap candidate for high-volume Construct batch work (alt text, classification); for PRAGMA, a new model tier to re-measure client visibility against.

2. **Baselayer raises $35M Series A (M13) and launches 'Know Your Agent' identity for AI agents** ([source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/22/with-35m-in-series-a-funding-baselayer-is-building-the-trust-layer-for-the-agentic-economy/)) — Announced 22 Sep: the bank identity-verification vendor's Agentic Identity Suite checks who deployed an agent, who it represents and whether it may transact. **Applicable: MAYBE** — Signals that agent identity/authorization is becoming buyer-facing infrastructure; a PRAGMA talking point for clients letting agents act on their behalf. No direct B9 use.

3. **arXiv 2609.15906: Authorization Architectures for Tool-Using AI Agents** ([source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15906)) — September paper laying out a principal hierarchy (user, operator, orchestrator, sub-agent, tool endpoint) and five layers: identity/credentials, delegation and scope propagation, runtime enforcement, prompt injection as authorization bypass, and auditability. **Applicable: YES** — A ready checklist for The Construct's credential-bearing skills and Hermes loops: scope what each sub-agent inherits and log it.

4. **Ema raises $77M Series B for 'AI employees' in enterprise HR, IT and finance** ([source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/23/ema-raises-77m-in-funding-to-deploy-ai-employees-across-enterprise-hr-it-and-finance-departments/)) — Announced 23 Sep (Creagis lead, Accel returning): Ema's prebuilt agents plan and execute tasks inside existing enterprise systems and escalate sensitive actions to a human approver; it cites Wipro and Hitachi deployments. **Applicable: MAYBE** — Validates PRAGMA's packaged-operator pitch (prebuilt role agents, not DIY frameworks) and the approve-before-sensitive-action pattern The Construct already uses; no direct B9 use.

5. **Rabbit launches OS3, a cloud personal agent that drives your own computers, imports skills from other agents, and pairs to your phone over Telegram** ([source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/23/rabbit-returns-with-os3-a-personal-ai-agent-that-can-access-files-and-connect-computers/)) — Unveiled 23 Sep: bring-your-own-key and model-agnostic (Claude, GPT, OpenRouter or local), installs on up to five Windows/macOS/Linux machines with one command, swaps models without losing memory or skills, and requires user confirmation for sensitive actions. SiliconANGLE names Hermes Agent among its direct comparables. **Applicable: MAYBE** — It is the same shape as The Construct (Hermes on the Mac, Telegram to Bobby's phone, skills as portable assets); worth watching whether "import a skill by URL" becomes a cross-agent standard, since that would make Construct skills sellable PRAGMA deliverables. No B9 use.

**PRAXIS lesson:** Three of today's five items are about agents acting with borrowed authority, and a fourth (Rabbit OS3) is a consumer product built the same way The Construct is: model-agnostic, skills as portable assets, consent on sensitive actions. As models get cheaper and swappable, the durable assets are the skills and the permission scoping around them, not the model underneath.
