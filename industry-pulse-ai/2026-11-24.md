**PRAXIS lesson:** Anthropic and Google both shipped the same shape this week: the agent proposes, and a separate reviewer (a PR agent or a judge model) checks the change before it lands. The Construct should route every sentinel fix and every send/publish through one reviewed path, so speed comes from the agents and safety comes from the gate.
