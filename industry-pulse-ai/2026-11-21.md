**PRAXIS lesson:** This week the big platforms converged on three patterns at once: desktop agents (Claude and Gemini both drive the Mac now), one gated, logged path for every tool call, and agents that propose fixes for a human to approve rather than acting alone. For The Construct, the drivers are becoming interchangeable, so invest in the gate (own identity per worker, one logged MCP gateway) and in fix-as-PR loops for the sentinel agents, not in any one vendor's agent.
