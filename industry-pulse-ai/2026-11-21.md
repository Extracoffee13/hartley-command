# Industry Pulse — AI / Agent Ecosystem · 2026-11-21

Daily scan for The Construct. Source URL · one-line summary · applicable to The Construct. (Refreshed 09:55 ET: added Gemini for Mac computer use and OpenAI Performance Factory.)

1. **1Password for Agents: agents sign in to sites without ever seeing the password, with a Claude integration and approvals that expire after 30 days (week of Nov 23)** ([1password.com](https://1password.com/personal-ai-agent-integrations)) — 1Password now brokers logins for AI agents so the agent gets a session, not the secret; its Claude integration adds an Agentic Mode, and each grant lapses after 30 days unless renewed. **Applicable: YES** — The Construct: Hermes, Cowork and the B9 partner-order runner currently drive logged-in sessions (Signs365, WordPress, Google) with credentials held in browser profiles and files; moving those logins behind a broker gives per-agent grants, expiry and revocation without pasting passwords into prompts. PRAGMA: the cleanest answer to a client's first question, "what happens to our passwords?"

2. **Google gives the Gemini Mac app computer use (Nov 19): it can operate Mac apps, fill forms and organize local files** ([blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/computer-use-mac/)) — Google's Gemini app for Mac can now carry out multi-step tasks across desktop apps and websites, starting in English for US Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers. **Applicable: MAYBE** — The Construct: with Claude computer use opening to Team/Enterprise the same week, desktop agents are now a two-vendor commodity, so the moat is our runbooks and gates (Signs365 cart steps, vendor portals), not the driver; keep runner tasks vendor-neutral. Brand 9 Signs: no-API vendor portals become automatable on either stack. PRAGMA: clients will ask which desktop agent to pick, so have a side-by-side ready.

3. **Wells Fargo changes its online access agreement (effective Nov 24): customers use third-party AI agents at their own risk; JPMorgan, Schwab and Citi already block or limit credential-based agents** ([killtheai.com](https://killtheai.com/news/top-10-ai-news-today-november-23-2026/)) — Big US banks are pushing liability for agent logins onto customers and shutting out agents that sign in with a customer's username and password. **Applicable: YES** — Brand 9 Signs: any agent task touching business banking (reconciliation, payments, pulling statements) should go through bank APIs, exports or a bookkeeping connector, not a scripted login; a credential-based bank session may now void protections. PRAGMA: a dated, concrete risk to put in every client's agent-permissions policy.

4. **SWE-CoWork benchmark: frontier coding agents pass about 63% of tests inside a simulated company but fully finish under 8% of assignments** ([explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/swe-cowork-coding-agents-digital-employees-benchmark-2026)) — A new benchmark drops 11 models into a simulated workplace; Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra clear most individual checks yet rarely complete a whole assignment end to end. **Applicable: YES** — The Construct: matches what our own runs show: partial progress is common, full completion is not. Grade agents on finished, verified deliverables (the done-means-done gate), not on steps passed, and keep a human checkpoint before anything ships. PRAGMA: a sober data point for setting client expectations on unattended agents.

5. **OpenAI details Performance Factory (Nov 19): Codex agents diagnose ChatGPT slowdowns and propose fixes for engineers to approve** ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/how-openai-performance-factory-keeps-chatgpt-fast/)) — OpenAI describes an internal system where Codex agents investigate latency regressions in ChatGPT, find the cause and propose fixes, with engineers reviewing before anything ships; Databricks shipped the same pattern for data jobs as Genie ZeroOps the same day. **Applicable: YES** — The Construct: two vendors converged on 'agent diagnoses, proposes a fix as a PR, human approves', which is the right template for the site-health sentinel and scheduled-task triage agents (they should open a fix, not just report). PRAGMA: a citable, primary-source example of safe self-healing ops. Brand 9 Signs: brand9signs.com site repairs via the sentinel.

**PRAXIS lesson:** This week the big platforms converged on three patterns at once: desktop agents (Claude and Gemini both drive the Mac now), one gated, logged path for every tool call, and agents that propose fixes for a human to approve rather than acting alone. For The Construct, the drivers are becoming interchangeable, so invest in the gate (own identity per worker, one logged MCP gateway) and in fix-as-PR loops for the sentinel agents, not in any one vendor's agent.
