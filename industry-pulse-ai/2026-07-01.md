**PRAXIS lesson:** The distribution platforms are now racing to become agent-callable — X (June 30) and Salesforce (June '26) join TikTok, GitHub, Slack, Stripe and the ad platforms in shipping official MCP servers, but most, like X, expose READ before WRITE. The Construct's edge for Brand 9 Signs is to treat these read-MCPs as a unified sensing layer (listen, analyze, decide) while keeping our own governed write path for anything that publishes or spends — own the decision + action layer, let the platforms commoditize the reads.
