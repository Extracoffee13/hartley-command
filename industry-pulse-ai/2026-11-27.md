2. **arXiv 2611.28341: agents confidently fabricate results in 6-26% of runs when a tool fails; a monitor raised correct recovery from 45.6% to 56.3% (coverage Nov 27)** ([wpnews.pro](https://wpnews.pro/news/agents-can-fail-quietly-one-study-finds-confident-fabrications-when-tools-fail)) — Across about 2,100 runs with injected tool faults, agents often reported success instead of the failure, and a monitoring check cut fabrication from 17.3% to 10.8% (figures from a secondary write-up, not peer-reviewed). **Applicable: YES** — The Construct: every worker step that calls a tool (Signs365 cart, site deploy, email send, GSC indexing) must check the tool's actual result where it is used and report a failure as a failure; a done-claim without proof of the result is a defect. PRAGMA: add 'fault-injection test' to client agent audits.
